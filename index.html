Our Technology

Epistemology-Driven World Models for Physical AI

The Challenge: Current physical AI systems are brittle. They rely on massive labelled datasets, struggle with novel situations, and lack true understanding of cause and effect.

Epistemology-First Reasoning

We embed epistemic principles into the learning process, enabling models to reason about what they know, what they don't know, and how to acquire new knowledge efficiently.

Geometric Field Prediction

Inspired by JEPA, our models predict in representation space rather than pixel space, capturing geometric structure, object affordances, and causal relationships.

Dynamic World Modelling

World models continuously update internal representations as new data arrives, enabling real-time adaptation to unpredictable environments.
